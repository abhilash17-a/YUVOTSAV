EVENT DETAILS

Event Overview

Create and perform an engaging skit that entertains, communicates an idea, or leaves the audience with something to think about.

Category: Group Performance
Minimum Team Size: 3 participants
Venue: SC AUDITORIUM, D-BLOCK
Time: 3:00 PM
Entry Fee: ₹100 per team
Registration: Register Now
RULES & CRITERIA

Rule Book

  • Each team must have a minimum of 3 participants.
  • The skit should be original and prepared by the participating team.
  • The performance must be respectful and suitable for a college cultural event.
  • No content that insults or disrespects any religion, community or cultural identity.
  • No offensive, obscene, discriminatory or abusive content.
  • No political propaganda, personal accusations or targeted content.
  • Props and costumes must be safe and appropriate for the venue.
  • Teams are responsible for bringing and removing their own props after the performance.
  • Judging will consider acting, storytelling, creativity, dialogue delivery, coordination and overall impact.
  • Judges' decision will be final.

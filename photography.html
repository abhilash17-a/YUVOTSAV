EVENT DETAILS

Event Overview

Capture the colours, traditions and moments of Navarathri through your lens and showcase your perspective at Navarang 2026.

Theme: Navarathri & Cultural Celebrations
Participation: Individual Only
Entry Fee: ₹100
Submission: Online
Last Date: 14 October 2026
Event & Winner Announcement: 16 October 2026, 3:00 PM
Venue: SC Auditorium
Submission Window: Entries can be uploaded anytime from now until 14 October 2026.
RULES & CRITERIA

Rule Book

  • Participation is individual only; each participant may submit only one entry.
  • The photograph must be captured by the participant and must relate to the theme Navarathri & Cultural Celebrations.
  • AI-generated images are strictly not allowed.
  • Both the RAW file and edited image must be submitted.
  • Editing is permitted for colour grading and basic adjustments only. Heavy manipulation or AI-based editing is not permitted.
  • Both files must follow the naming format: NAME_USN_RAW and NAME_USN_EDITED.
  • Entries can be submitted anytime before 14 October 2026.
  • Late submissions will not be considered.
  • Participants must ensure that the submitted photograph does not contain offensive, discriminatory or disrespectful content.
  • Photographs must not intentionally insult or disrespect any religion, community, individual or cultural identity.
  • The photograph should not contain content that is politically provocative, defamatory or intended to target any individual or group.
  • The participant must have the right to submit the photograph and should not submit someone else's work.
  • Judging may consider composition, creativity, storytelling, lighting, relevance to the theme and overall visual impact.
  • Judges' decision will be final.

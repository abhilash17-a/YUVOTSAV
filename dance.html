RULES & CRITERIA

Rule Book

  • Solo and group entries are allowed; groups must have at least 3 members.
  • Performances must be respectful and suitable for a college cultural event.
  • No content that insults or disrespects any religion, community or cultural identity.
  • No offensive, obscene, discriminatory or abusive content.
  • No political propaganda, personal accusations or targeted content.
  • Dangerous props or materials that may cause injury or damage are prohibited.
  • Participants must manage and remove their own props after performing.
  • Judging will consider choreography, coordination, expression, creativity and stage presence.
  • Judges' decision will be final.
